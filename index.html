THE COMMUNITY

Quietly connected.

Shrevecrest sits between Vienna and Falls Church, adjacent to Idylwood Park and the W&OD Trail. It is close to Tysons and major commuter routes, yet feels distinctly tucked away.

OUR HISTORY

Our story began in 1971.

Shrevecrest was planned by architect A.G. Mumma and built by R.J.L. Associates as a rare contemporary subdivision in Fairfax County. Forty of the forty-two homes comprised three model types: the Adventure, the Chaparral, and the Dorado, each rotated at varied angles to create visual interest.

"Shrevecrest is radiant with serene natural beauty you thought no longer existed in our busy world. The loveliness of peaceful pasturelands, cool green splendor of large trees shading your home and neighborhood streets, the priceless luxury of still summer evenings."

-- Original 1971 sales brochure
